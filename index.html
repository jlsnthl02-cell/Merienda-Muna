About us

A café with a home kitchen's heart.

We make coffee, hot chocolate and milkshakes, and bake the breads, pastries and merienda favorites many of us grew up with, from pandesal in the morning to buko pie at the table.

Every bake is made fresh for its date, and every drink is made when you collect it.

Good to know

Questions

How far ahead should I order?

Drinks can be ready the same day, so a coffee or shake order can be for today. Breads, pastries and merienda need 1 day. Pies and cakes need 2 days. The date picker only offers dates that fit your order.

How do I send my order?

Review your order here, take a screenshot of the order slip, and send it to our Facebook page on Messenger. Your order is confirmed once we reply.

How do I pay?

Nothing is charged on this site. After you send your order, we confirm the total and share how to pay.

What about allergies?

Tell us about any allergy in the notes box when you order. Our kitchen uses wheat, milk, eggs and nuts.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Message us on Facebook as early as you can. Changes are easiest before we start baking or making your drinks.
